Hermes Personal Assistant

A private, single-user assistant that runs on its owner's own computer at home.

What it is

Hermes is a personal tool, not a public service. It is used by one person to check their own Gmail and Google Calendar. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, anyone else.

What it does with Google

  • Reads and searches the owner's email to summarize it on request.
  • Archives email the owner asks to archive (removes it from the Inbox; nothing is deleted).
  • Reads the owner's calendars and adds events the owner asks for.

It never sends email, deletes email, or edits or deletes calendar events.

Where data goes

Nowhere else. Sign-in tokens and all data stay on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to third parties. Details are in the privacy policy.
